Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Tithi Galleries.

1. What is Tithi Galleries?

Tithi Galleries is an India-first photo gallery platform for wedding and event photographers. You can create event galleries, upload photos, and share a live link or QR code so guests can view and find their photos easily.

2. How do I create a gallery?

Sign up, go to your Dashboard, enter the event name, date, and description, then click Create Gallery. You’ll get a shareable gallery link and QR code instantly.

3. Can I connect my camera and upload photos automatically?

Yes — in the current web app, you can use Camera to Cloud (Live) from the Dashboard. On your phone, tap “Open Camera & Upload”, capture a photo, and it uploads to the selected gallery live. Direct DSLR/mirrorless WiFi tether auto-upload is on the roadmap.

4. Can I upload photos manually?

Yes. From the Dashboard, you can upload photos from your device or paste an image URL. Manual uploads appear in the gallery immediately.

5. Do guests need to install an app?

No. Guests simply open the gallery link or scan the QR code on any phone browser. The gallery is mobile-friendly for Android and iPhone.

6. Can guests find their photos quickly?

Yes. Guests can search by keywords in the gallery. AI face-recognition selfie search is planned in upcoming phases, so guests will be able to find all photos they appear in even faster.

7. How does Tithi make money?

Tithi’s planned revenue model is subscription plans for photographers (monthly/yearly, India-friendly pricing) plus optional commission on online store sales. This keeps the core gallery affordable while supporting premium features like storage, AI search, and business tools.

8. Is this ready for Android and iPhone?

The web app is mobile-friendly and works in Android and iPhone browsers today. We built the full workflow in the web app first so it can be tested now, and it can be wrapped into Android/iOS apps later.
